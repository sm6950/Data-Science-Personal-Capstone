Introduction to Generalized Additive Models (GAMs)

GAMs extend traditional regression by modeling nonlinear relationships with smooth functions.

  • Introduced by Hastie & Tibshirani (1986, 1990)
  • Built on Generalized Linear Models (GLMs)
  • Balance flexibility and interpretability
  • Used in finance, healthcare, auditing, and cybersecurity
  • Enable transparent, stakeholder-friendly modeling
